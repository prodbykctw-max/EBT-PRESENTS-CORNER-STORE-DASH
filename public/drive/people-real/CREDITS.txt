The pedestrians of the realistic look (people-b.js; every person is one of these): body shapes, skin weights, the walk and the shoes are derived from MakeHuman data.

Source:   https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman   (commit a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482)

Files used (all under makehuman/data/ in that repository):
  3dobjs/base.obj                       the base body mesh (the "body" group only)
  rigs/default.mhskel                   joint positions
  rigs/default_weights.mhw              skin weights
  targets/macrodetails/*.target         gender / age / weight / muscle shapes (blended into six body types)

Shoes (clothes assets, CC0), fitted to each body with their .mhclo recipes (only the mesh and the fit are used; no textures, no skin weights):
  shoes02   the sneaker      from makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip   "This asset was explicitly released as CC0 in september 2020"
                              (Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier; same statement as the body data above)
  shoes01   the lace-up dress shoe   same pack and statement
  toigo_ankle_boots_male   the ankle boot (Boots_Ankle_Male, author MRT / Margaret Toigo)   from shoes01_cc0.zip; its .mhclo says "# license CC0"
  https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html , https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/shoes01.html
  (the asset-pack pages list both packs as CC0). The sock tube that the two system shoes come with is cut off above the ankle; the colours are painted by the shader
  (no brand marks anywhere). Fetched by drive/tools/people/fetch_shoes.py (HTTP range requests, generic User-Agent).

What is NOT from MakeHuman: the hair, caps, backpacks, hoods, skirts, the painted clothes and faces and the walk cycle itself. Those are made by this
project (drive/tools/people/export_shells.mjs, drive/tools/people/build_people.py, public/drive/people-alt.js). Nothing here comes from Mixamo or
any other download. MakeHuman's hair and skins were not used, and of its clothes only the three shoes above.

people2.json and people2.bin are generated (people.json and people.bin are the previous build, kept unchanged so a tab still running the old code finds its matching data):
  python3 -I drive/tools/people/fetch_shoes.py --out <shoe dir>
  python3 -I drive/tools/people/build_people.py --mh <checkout>/makehuman/data --shoes <shoe dir> --out public/drive/people-real
(needs numpy, scipy and bpy for Blender's decimate modifier; the build takes a few seconds).
