Files used (all under makehuman/data/ in that repository):
  3dobjs/base.obj                       the base body mesh (the "body" group only)
  rigs/default.mhskel                   joint positions
  rigs/default_weights.mhw              skin weights
  targets/macrodetails/*.target         gender / age / weight / muscle shapes (blended into six body types)

What is NOT from MakeHuman: the hair, caps, backpacks, hoods, skirts, the painted clothes and faces and the walk cycle itself. Those are made by this
project (drive/tools/people/export_shells.mjs, drive/tools/people/build_people.py, public/drive/people-alt.js). Nothing here comes from Mixamo or
any other download. MakeHuman's clothes, hair and skins were not used.

people.json and people.bin are generated: python3 -I drive/tools/people/build_people.py --mh <checkout>/makehuman/data --out public/drive/people-real
(needs numpy, scipy and bpy for Blender's decimate modifier; the build takes a few seconds).
