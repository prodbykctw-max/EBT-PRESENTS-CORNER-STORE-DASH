The animals of the realistic look (public/drive/animals.js): the stray dogs and the pigeons. Built by drive/tools/animals/build_animals.py (+ build_pigeon.py).
With ?animals=none (or in the comic look) none of these files is loaded and the props.glb dogs and birds are drawn.

DOG   dog.webp, dog_n.webp, and the dog's mesh / skeleton / trot, run, idle and sniff poses in animals.bin
      "PitBull" by Joseph AYYAD (Sketchfab @Joseph.AD)   https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/pitbull-2be37d5b9f754ed4bcc2d8f41b23a1d7
      Licence: CC Attribution 4.0   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
      CHANGES MADE HERE: scaled to the game's dog size and turned to face -z; the loose debris / claw specks around the paws were left out; the normal map was downscaled to
      1024 px WebP (the colour texture is kept at 2048 px); the poses were baked into a bone-matrix texture (animals.json / animals.bin); the coat colour is changed per dog in the shader (the white markings,
      skin, nose and eyes keep their own colours). The "Armature.008|Walkl", "IDdle" and "Eat" animations of the source are what the dog plays; the walk and run are the source's Walkl re-timed into a trot with a longer swing at the hip (drive/tools/animals/gait.py).

PIGEON  pigeon.webp, and the pigeon's mesh / skeleton / poses in animals.bin
      "Pigeon" by kenchoo (Sketchfab)   https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/pigeon-ddd5ef4a94eb4159937a9de25c45697c
      Licence: CC Attribution 4.0   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
      which is itself based on "Pigeon #1" by NMC BC (Sketchfab)   https://skfb.ly/oQsJv   (CC Attribution)
      CHANGES MADE HERE: scaled and turned to face -z; the texture is the source's own image downscaled to 1024 px WebP; a second, cheaper level of detail (900 triangles);
      the source has no wings (they are painted on a folded body), so the flying pigeon's two wings are NEW geometry made by build_pigeon.py (flat feather strips textured
      with the feather patches of the source texture) with four new joints; the pecking and flying poses are made here by turning joint groups of the source skeleton
      (legs trailed back in flight, body pitched and neck folded down to peck). The standing and idle pigeon is the source model with the source's idle animation.

Credit line on the title screen (standalone/shell2.html): "Dog: "PitBull" by Joseph AYYAD, pigeon: "Pigeon" by kenchoo after NMC BC (CC BY 4.0), modified".
