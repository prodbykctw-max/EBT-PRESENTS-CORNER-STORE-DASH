Changes made here: only the milk carton and the beans can are taken from Long Life Food; its texture keeps only their parts
(the tomato and sardine labels are dropped), and the made-up brand marks are painted out (the carton's "Pecina's" ribbon, cow
badge and the side-panel text naming it; the can's "Pezor's BEST BEANS" starburst), so no brand name or logo is in the game.
The three rolls of Hamburger Buns are pushed together into a cluster. Meshes simplified with meshoptimizer, textures resized to
512 px (normal maps 256 px) WebP, geometry meshopt-compressed.

Made here (no outside model; public/store3d/pickups.js, procedural, no brands): the roast chicken in its black tray (CHICKEN),
the chips bag (CHIPS), the lemonade bottle (LEMONADE) and, from products.js, the rice sack (RICE & BEANS). No CC0 / CC BY
roast chicken, drumstick, chips bag or drink bottle was downloadable from the sources this build could reach (Poly Haven has
none; Sketchfab's CC BY food models need a signed-in account to download).
